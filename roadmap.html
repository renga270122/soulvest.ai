Roadmap

This roadmap distinguishes what is already public on Soulvest.ai from what is still being tested, documented, or expanded in pilot. It is intended to set accurate expectations for users and platform reviewers.

Phase 1: Live Now

  • Public landing site with trust, contact, and privacy pages.
  • Live links to Soulvest Music, SoulSkill, and SoulWell.
  • Basic indexing support through robots and sitemap files.

Phase 2: Pilot Expansion

  • Resident benefits articulation and commune positioning pages.
  • Limited rollout of companion tools and community workflows.
  • Founder-led refinement of trust signals, messaging, and credibility content.

Phase 3: Operational Readiness

  • Google Workspace setup once domain legitimacy and review signals are sufficient.
  • Email authentication with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
  • Search Console verification, indexing growth, and broader visibility.

Phase 4: Community Maturity

  • Expanded resident experiences, rituals, and support structures.
  • Stronger partner communication, testimonials, and public updates.
  • A more complete ecosystem bridging products, programs, and communal growth.
